About

Management and technology student at TU Munich, drawn to the intersection of physical form, engineering, and how objects come to exist.

Raised at the International School of Düsseldorf, I grew up between cultures — German, Danish, and international — which shaped an early curiosity about how things are made and why they look the way they do. I'm currently in my sixth semester at the Technical University of Munich, specialising in Mechanical Engineering alongside Management.

Outside of university, I've worked across start-ups, hospitality, and international business — experiences that have given me an appreciation for how good design serves real people in real contexts. This portfolio is a record of the objects and ideas I've been building on the side.
